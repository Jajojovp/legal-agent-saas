- **Projected $6.5 billion** by 2028 (43% CAGR)

- ✅ **Labor shortage:** Paralegals and admin staff increasingly expensive
- ✅ **Client expectations:** 73% of clients expect 24/7 availability
- ✅ **Competition:** Firms using AI report 34% higher client satisfaction
- ✅ **ROI:** Average 180% ROI on legal tech investments

**Post-COVID Shift:**
- **89%** of law firms now use cloud-based software (up from 48% in 2019)

- **Shorter sales cycles:** 45 days → 21 days

- **Self-service preferred:** 71% research online before contacting sales

4. **Long sales cycles:** 60-90 days average

**Solution Demand:**
- **94%** want automated lead capture
- **87%** want lead scoring/qualification
- **81%** want automated follow-up
- **76%** want integration with existing tools
